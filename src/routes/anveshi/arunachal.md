Imagine an Indian state with natural and cultural diversity as breathtaking as to contain two of the eight biodiversity hotspots in the world, and to have 118 different tribes with each speaking a different language. It boasts the longest Himalayan stretch in India, and the only national park in the entire world which spans thick tropical rainforests and snow capped peaks. And a society deeply connected to the Sanatana roots of Bharatavarsha. We speak of Arunachal Pradesh, dubbed by Prime Minister Modi as having the ‘first village’ of India. This Anveshi, come with us to see where the Brahmaputra meets the plains, at the place where Lord Parashuram absolved himself of the sin of killing his mother, making it the Kumbh of north-east. This is where ancient Hindu kings of Bhismakanagar ruled.

Where Hindus built temples in as early as the 10th century. The region considered the maika of Rukmini. It faces many challenges from missionaries and yet battles on; and its gods, like Rangfrah, Rangjouban and Donyi-Polo, occupy prominent cultural space. This Anveshi, you will also get to see Naga Vaishnava communities and their last hideouts in the mountains. You will get a masterclass on why the north-east is what it is now, and how coal, oil and tea were the factors which the British used to deeply transform the region. Come with us on this Anveshi to take a leap of imagination, adventure, culture, religion and history, along with the staple Anveshi architecture and sculpture. This Anveshi, we're upping the game!
