Traveling through one of the most pristine states of Bharatavarsha, you will see the vibrancy of both culture and nature. Along with magnificent temples in different nagara styles, built in pink sandstone, you will encounter pristine beaches, remote forests, quiet countryside and the breathtaking vistas of eastern India.

This chapter of Bodha Anveshi will show you the architectural and sculptural splendor, and the flowering, of a sub-branch of the nagara style vimanas. We will immerse ourselves in the uniting and liberating cultural ethos which Sanatana dharma is capable of, infused in remote corners of our country.
