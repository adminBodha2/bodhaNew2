# Source Summaries

This page summarizes every raw journal issue in `raw/` from Vol 1, No. 1 through Vol 11, No. 1. It complements source-index, which lists article titles, by giving each issue a concise thematic digest and linking it to the main wiki topics.

## Volume 1

### Vol 1, No. 1 (January 2016)

The opening issue establishes several enduring tracks of the journal: copper-hoards in Meerut and Baraut collections, numismatics from Sitapur, epigraphy-and-inscriptions at Kalinjar, temple-architecture at Nilkantha, and medieval-monuments through Lodi and Mughal structures. It sets the journal's pattern of combining museum catalogues, field documentation, and monument study.

### Vol 1, No. 2 (April 2016)

This issue centers on excavated and surveyed landscapes: Hetapatti, Naimisharanya, Nalanda, Sitapur, Chitrakut, Bari, and Lucknow. It is foundational for excavation-reports, regional-surveys, sacred-geography, numismatics, temple-architecture, and heritage-management concerns in urban monument settings.

### Vol 1, No. 3 (July 2016)

The issue extends the north Indian fieldwork emphasis with Barood Khera, Sitapur, Agiabir, Basadila Tilatar, Kaimur inscriptions, Jageshwar temples, Salabat Khan's tomb, and virtual archaeology. It is especially important for the OCP field link in copper-hoards, multi-period excavation-reports, epigraphy-and-inscriptions, and temple-architecture.

### Vol 1, No. 4 (October 2016)

This issue widens the corpus into Indus-border and sacred-art questions: Khanak, Siyapur, millets in Indus peripheries, Jain Deogarh, Ajmer monuments, and Mughal gardens. It contributes to indus-valley-civilization, archaeobotany-palaeoenvironment, art-iconography-and-sculpture, temple-architecture, and medieval-monuments.
