Shri Ram Swarup was one of the finest thinkers of Bharatavarsha, who commented upon every important aspect of Hindu culture; and upon the nature of Bharata's civilizational enemies, with penetrative insights. He did a purva paksha of ideologies like Islam, Christianity, Judaism and Communism, through an exposition of yogic chitta bhumis. He explained yoga sadhana and its centrality to Hindu Dharma, drawing from deep meditational experiences. He explained Hindu darshana and nuances of language, words, and roots by discussing names of gods.

He dreamed of re-establishing Sanatana Dharma as a universal tradition, while helping pagan sects to revive. Never wasting a single word, his writing is un-editable like a sutra, and yet exudes great literary beauty. Shri Ram Swarup was a deep civilizational thinker — spiritually, intellectually, and culturally. Every aspiring thinker and leader should heed his call for Hindu renaissance, and his concepts of Svayambodha and Shatrubodha. This course is an introduction to the work of Shri Ram Swarup, discussing all his works in eight lectures, leading the learners to a better understanding of Bharatavarsha and the world.
