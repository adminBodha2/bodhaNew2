# Bodha Hero Image Prompts

These prompts are written for hero images. Each prompt gives the image model concrete people, places, objects, gestures, spatial structure, and symbolic anchors. The prompts stay style-agnostic so the same content can be rendered in different visual treatments.

## General Bodha Website Key Image

Setting: A wide hero scene that brings together books, fieldwork, temple culture, maps, research diagrams, and public thought. The scene is set in a large Indian research studio whose back wall opens into a layered landscape of Bharatavarsha: river, temple spire, village path, trees, and a distant city skyline. The image communicates serious inquiry, cultural confidence, and patient institution-building.
Characters in the scene: seven contemporary Indian researchers and cultural workers, men and women from their late twenties to fifties, brown South Asian skin, dressed in cotton kurtas, saris, shawls, and simple shirts. One senior scholar studies a palm-leaf manuscript facsimile beside printed books. A younger researcher marks a map of sacred geography on the wall. Two field researchers review temple photographs and village-interview transcripts on a long table. One woman scholar arranges a policy brief beside a hand-drawn institutional diagram. A designer studies a dharmic design canvas beside sketches of objects, interfaces, and community spaces. Everyone is absorbed in work.
Main composition: a long wooden table runs across the foreground, covered with open books, field journals, archival printouts, temple plans, village maps, pencils, a small brass diya, a laptop with a clean knowledge graph, and photographs from temple journeys. The middle ground holds people in active work. The background carries the map, bookshelves, research pinboards, and the open landscape. Leave a quieter band of wall, sky, or open window light on one side for website title overlay.
