Bodha is a think tank and research group studying contemporary issues of cultural concern. We inform core areas of policy with wisdom derived from Hindu culture and philosophy, and help orient education to the Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS). We also curate cultural experiences of core Hindu institutions like the Hindu temple.

## Our Mandate
1. Provide an Indian cultural lens to foreign policy and institutional design, with original research in frontier areas.
2. Bring leading Hindu minds together by asking pertinent questions about most critical cultural problems and creating a bank of actionable solutions.
3. Create immersive cultural experiences to make our youth more culture aware and active.

## Work
Research is our primary work stream, with projects in current and upcoming pipelines. We point methodological rigor, authenticity, and emic cultural sensitivity towards issues such as Hindu models of temple management, Hindu views of modernity, and more. 

- [Research Projects](/research)

With the [Big Questions](/big-questions) initiative, we're asking provocative questions about the most fundamental problems and open questions that Hindu society faces today.
We also operate [Bodha Academy](/academy) to create a line of scholars capable of carrying the Hindu intellectural work forward.

Bodha Anveshi is our flagship cultural immersion program, curating sacred tours to unexplored cultural kshetras and trails.
Through these essays and videos you can engage deeper with our intellectual output, vision, and views on culture and tradition.

## About Us
No work emerges in isolation. Every idea, method, and conviction is shaped by a longer continuum of thought, by thinkers who asked difficult questions, and by traditions that refined ways of seeing the world.
