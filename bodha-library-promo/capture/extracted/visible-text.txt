[h1] Bodha Open Library
[p] A collection of readings in Hindu culture and history, philosophical systems, Indian knowledge systems (IKS), scriptures, and more.
[p] Bodha Open Library is a collection of readings in Hindu culture and history, philosophical systems, Indian knowledge systems (IKS), scriptures, and more. Find your next reading by browsing the categories, or select one of our curated reading paths.
[p] All texts in the library are sourced from the public domain. If any text violates copyright, please write to us at
[span] sitemaster@bodharesearch.in
[p] . All works compiled under 'Aryan Issue' are externally hosted/published papers, and links will open in a new tab.

[p] Scriptural

[p] Shatrubodha
[p] 20 texts
[p] Texts dealing with critique, conflict, ideology, and civilisational self-understanding.
[p] Svayambodha
[p] 21 texts
[p] Books oriented toward self-understanding, cultural continuity, and Hindu civilisational confidence.
[p] Indian Archaeology
[p] 41 issues
[p] The Indian Journal of Archaeology (IJA) has made public each of their journal issues since 2016.
[p] Arts, Performance, and Aesthetics
[p] Sources on music, drama, performance, rasa, kala, and the theory and practice of artistic experience.
[p] Darshanas and Philosophy
[p] Works on the major schools of Indian philosophy, including Nyaya, Vaisheshika, Sankhya, Yoga, Mimamsa, Vedanta, and Shaiva thought.
[p] Dharma, Ethics, Law
[p] Texts dealing with duty, moral order, legal reasoning, social norms, ritual obligations, and the dharmashastra tradition.
[p] Hinduism Overview
[p] Broad introductory works that summarize Hindu tradition as a whole, including its beliefs, practices, worldview, and civilizational continuity.
[p] History, Polity, and Society
[p] Works on political thought, governance, social structure, chronicles, institutions, and historical memory in Indian civilization.
[p] Indian Knowledge Systems
[p] Texts on traditional systems of organized knowledge, including science, mathematics, technical disciplines, applied learning, and knowledge classification.
[p] Indology and Civilizational Critique
[p] Modern critical works examining Indology, orientalism, academic method, and the ideological framing of Indian traditions and civilization.
[p] Language, Logic, Sanskrit
[p] Works on Sanskrit language, grammar, semantics, lexicography, linguistic philosophy, and systems of logical reasoning.
[p] Puranic and Itihasic Literature
[p] Narrative and sacred-historical texts such as the Puranas, epics, and story literature preserving cosmology, myth, and sacred geography.
[p] Vedic and Upanishadic Texts
[p] Sources centered on the Vedas, Rigvedic interpretation, and Upanishads, especially themes of revelation, ritual, self, brahman, and liberation.
[p] Bharata1000 is a curation of 1000 books to learn and understand itihasa, Bharata, and Dharma. These books are not available here at the library, but several that are in the public domain are linked to their sources, and for those in copyright we label so. The idea here is to provide rampways and learning paths for those looking to study deeper into civilizational and cultural Bharata.
					Presenting a work in this list is not an endorsement of its contents -
[p] ...for many are listed to inform the reader's shatrubodha, and expose them to problematic but well-known paradigms for India and Dharma. For example, there is no good English language translation of the Rigveda, and problems abound in those that are available. But if Ralph Griffith's work shows how the early European mind understood the text, and how they read it, then the more recent
					translation by Jamison and Brereton evidences the interpretations that are being pushed now.
