# Design System

## Overview

Bodha Open Library is a clean editorial research page built around a wide illustrated hero and structured reading grids. The page uses a white surface, black serif headlines, restrained sans-serif body text, and blue accents for active navigation and section arrows. Layout patterns are scholarly and organized: a fixed top navigation, a full-width cultural illustration, category grids, pill-like Bharata1000 section buttons, and resource cards.

## Colors

- **Primary Surface**: `#FFFFFF` - main page background and card surface.
- **Soft Surface**: `#FAFEFF` - near-white page wash and airy negative space.
- **Paper Warmth**: `#F9F8F6` - subtle book/page warmth from the captured design.
- **Primary Text**: `#070707` - headings and strong body copy.
- **Body Text**: `#474747` - paragraph and card descriptions.
- **Muted Text**: `#878787` - labels, counters, and secondary annotations.
- **Border Quiet**: `#DFDFDF` - grid lines and card separators.
- **Accent Blue**: `#1971C2` - active library nav, section arrows, and emphasis.
- **Deep Footer Blue**: `#0F172A` - closing frame depth and contrast.

## Typography

- **Serif Display**: Source Serif 4 (680-690 variable). Used for the page title and section headings; formal, literary, and research-oriented.
- **Serif Alternate**: EB Garamond and Newsreader. Useful for bookish accent text and quoted phrases.
- **Sans-Serif UI**: Google Sans and Google Sans Flex (400-500, variable). Used for navigation, body copy, category cards, and interface labels.
- **Captured Scale**: h1 around 52px, h2 around 33px, body and cards around 16-24px on the website; video may enlarge these while preserving hierarchy.

## Elevation

The page is mostly flat and border-defined rather than shadow-driven. Depth comes from the illustrated hero image, thin `#DFDFDF` dividers, white card grids, and the contrast between scholarly surface and deep blue footer. Motion design should add parallax and paper-layer movement without turning the site into a glossy SaaS dashboard.

## Components

- **Illustrated Knowledge Hero**: A full-width cultural illustration showing temples, books, a lamp, a reader, and classical imagery; this is the strongest signature asset.
- **Editorial Header**: Bodha logo at left, uppercase navigation at right, active Library link in `#1971C2`.
- **Collection Category Grid**: Eight white grid cells with title, count, and short explanation, separated by thin gray borders.
- **Reading Path Grid**: Topic cards such as Arts, Darshanas, Dharma, Hinduism Overview, IKS, Sanskrit, Puranic, and Vedic/Upanishadic texts.
- **Bharata1000 Topic Field**: Dense rows of small outlined category buttons, organized as a study map rather than a download library.
- **External Resource Cards**: White grid cards linking out to GRETIL, Sanskrit Documents, Digital Corpus of Sanskrit, Shodh Ganga, Archive.org, and dictionaries.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's

- Use white, warm paper, and deep blue surfaces with crisp black serif typography.
- Let the hero illustration carry cultural texture; use slow pans, zooms, and crops.
- Animate grids as organized research maps: cards cascade, counters count, sections draw in with blue arrows.
- Preserve the scholarly tone: precise labels, generous whitespace, and readable text.
- Use `#1971C2` sparingly but visibly for transitions, arrows, and focus markers.

### Don'ts

- Do not turn the page into a neon technology ad.
- Do not overuse gradients; if used, keep them localized and quiet.
- Do not replace the captured page with generic book imagery.
- Do not use heavy drop shadows or rounded bubbly cards.
- Do not make text-only beats; every beat should reference captured page structure or assets.
