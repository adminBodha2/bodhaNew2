What does the world look like?

Four visual treatments. Pick the one that fits Bodha's existing academic + design language.

A. Pixel-art retro

16-bit / Stardew-style. Chunky tiles, sprite avatar, item bubbles. Familiar, nostalgic, but fights Bodha's editorial tone.

B. Illustrated / painterly

Hand-drawn feel like Tunic or Chicory. Rich, but high asset cost and tone shift away from current site.

C. Line-art / diagrammatic matches Bodha

Clean strokes, sage/cream palette, sanskrit glyphs as items. Feels like an illuminated manuscript come to life.
