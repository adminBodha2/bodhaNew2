How big is the world on screen?

Two ways to fit a 5-biome world into the viewport. Pick the one that feels right.

A. Whole world visible, no scroll

One big map, all 5 biomes at once. Avatar is small but you can see the whole landscape. Feels like a god-view civilization map.

Grove L1
Riverbed L2

[avatar is small, you see everything]

B. Camera follows avatar, scrolls

One screen at a time, camera follows as you walk. Avatar is big and detailed. Feels like a real game; you discover biomes as you reach them.
