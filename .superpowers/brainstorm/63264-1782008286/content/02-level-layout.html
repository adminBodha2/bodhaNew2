How are the 5 levels organized inside the world?

All five sessions must be reachable from inside the world. How are they laid out?

A. Linear zones in one landscape

One continuous map. Zone 1 → Zone 2 → ... → Zone 5 as connected biomes. Progressing opens the next.

[Grove L1] → [Riverbed L2] → [Kiln-hill L3] → [Library L4] → [Sanctum L5]

B. Central hub + portals

A central home area with paths/portals to each level zone. Return to hub any time. Each level = its own space.

[Hub] — gate → [L1 Grove]   gate → [L2 Riverbed]   ...   gate → [L5 Sanctum]

C. One world, level-gated items

Single open map. You can walk anywhere from the start. Higher-level pickups are veiled/locked until you reach that level.

[Whole landscape visible]  ·  higher-tier items appear as you level up
