How does forging happen inside the world?

You said the bench lives inside the world. Where does the avatar actually combine items?

A. Approach forge → modal overlay

Walk to a forge structure in the biome. Press E. The existing /dhatu-forge panel opens as an overlay on the world. Reuses the current crafting UI 1:1.

[forge modal overlay]

B. In-world forge panel

Walk to the forge. A small crafting panel appears at the forge's location in the world (not full overlay). Pick dhatu + prefix from world inventory; result floats out into the world.

[in-world panel]

C. Open inventory, combine anywhere

No forge location. Press I to open inventory anywhere. Two slots inside, combine. World pauses while inventory is open. Lighter on world geometry; less ceremonial.

[inventory panel]
