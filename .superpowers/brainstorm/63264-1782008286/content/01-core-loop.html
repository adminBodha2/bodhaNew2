What's the core loop inside the world?

Walking and picking things up is the floor. What else happens between "found it" and "back to the bench"?

A. Pure exploration

Walk around, items sit on the map, walk over them to collect, walk back to the bench. Peaceful, focused on discovery.

Loop
step into world → walk to glowing pickup → collect → walk back to bench → craft

B. Puzzles to unlock pickups

Each item is behind a small gate: match a sandhyā pair, complete a sequence, slide a tile. Adds mental texture.

Loop
step in → see locked item → solve mini-puzzle → collect → back to bench

C. Encounters & obstacles

Pickup spots are guarded — dodge, time-window, or briefly answer a question. Adds tension and stakes.

Loop
step in → reach guarded spot → beat the encounter → collect → back to bench
